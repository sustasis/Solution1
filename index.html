How Quotes Work

Free Written On-Site Estimate vs. Online Initial Read

Every tree sits in a unique drop zone with different root stability, wood decay, and overhead obstacles. Here is how we handle requests accurately:

  • 1. Online / Text Photo Submission = Initial Read Only Sending tree details or photos online gives Angel a fast initial read on scope and equipment needs. It is not a formal arborist inspection or final quote.
  • 2. In-Person Walk-Through = Free Written Estimate Angel visits your property in person to check basal stability, drop zones, and access, then hands you a free written on-site estimate with debris haul-off included.
  • 3. Urgent Hazard? Skip the Form If a tree or heavy limb is actively splitting or resting on a roof, fence, or driveway, call (915) 243-9717 for 24/7 emergency response.

Straight Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a quote from photos, or do you need to see the tree in person?

You are welcome to describe the job or text photos for a quick initial read on scope, but an online or photo review is not an inspection. Because root flare health, internal decay, backyard gate width, and drop-zone hazards can only be verified in person, every binding quote is delivered as a free written on-site estimate.

Is debris haul-off included in your tree work?

Yes. Full debris haul-off and yard rake-down are standard on our pruning, trimming, and removal jobs. You won’t be left with piles of brush at the curb. If you want limbs or trunk wood cut and stacked for your own firewood, just let Angel know during the walk-through.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency tree removal?

Yes. High winds and monsoon storms in Canutillo and El Paso frequently split heavy limbs over roofs, driveways, and fences. Call (915) 243-9717 at any hour for emergency stabilization and removal.

Do you sell seasoned firewood year-round?

We stock split, seasoned firewood at our Canutillo yard (141 Dixie St) and offer local delivery across the Upper Valley and El Paso. Call or submit the form above to check current cord/bundle availability.
